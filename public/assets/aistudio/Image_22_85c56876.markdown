## Brand & Style
The design system powers an interactive, cinematic 3D animated web experience celebrating grassroots educational empowerment through the journey of Bharari—a vibrant blue-and-orange bird carrying a miniature leather schoolbag across rural Maharashtra. 

The aesthetic translates the wonder of prestige feature-length animation into an interactive canvas. It fuses organic nature-infused warmth with refined, cutting-edge digital craftsmanship. The design blends:
- **Atmospheric Storybook Realism:** Sun-drenched horizons, terracotta rooftops, whispering sugarcane foliage, and sweeping cinematic camera vistas.
- **Luminous Glassmorphism:** Translucent dialogue overlays, floating HUD progress markers, and misted ambient controls that feel suspended in dewy morning air.
- **Tactile Warmth:** Polished pill surfaces with subtle inner specular glows evoking morning light catching polished resin or wet river stone.

The emotional tone is deeply uplifting, reverent, adventurous, and prestige-grade. It invites children, educators, and global patrons alike to engage with educational transformation not as a clinical metric, but as an enchanting living narrative.

## Colors
The color palette draws directly from the biosphere and architectural soul of rural Maharashtra during golden hour:
- **Primary (`#D85A2A` - Sahyadri Saffron Terracotta):** Represents the morning sun breaking over red earth, Bharari's radiant plumage, and fiery optimism. Used for focal call-to-actions, vital narrative triggers, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#1A7B9B` - Monsoon Sky Teal):** Evokes crisp Deccan morning skies, Bharari's flight feathers, and clear water reserves. Used for narrative transitions, secondary interactive triggers, and key informational badges.
- **Tertiary (`#295A3E` - Deep Sugarcane Green):** Grounding vegetative shade, symbolizing growth, sustenance, and regional geography. Used for deep environmental frames, chapter markers, and subtle grounding accents.
- **Neutral (`#2B2621` - Roasted Earth Charcoal):** A rich, organic near-black imbued with warm umber undertones, ensuring high legibility without the sterile chill of pure carbon black.

### Supporting Background & Luminous Accents
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm milk and morning dew (`#FBF8F2` to `#F3EFE6`).
- **Solar Highlighting:** Golden nectar (`#F5B738`) used strictly for dynamic ambient light bleeding, focus rings, and hover glows.
- **Surface Translucency:** Frosted glass white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) balanced with soft warm tinting for dialogue balloons and floating interface panels.

## Typography
`Plus Jakarta Sans` governs the entire typographic hierarchy. Its humanist curves, open counters, and refined geometric underpinnings allow it to act simultaneously as a storybook display typeface and a crisp UI font. 

- Large titles leverage tighter negative tracking and bold weights to evoke modern animated title cards.
- Narrative prose maintains generous line heights for frictionless reading against dynamic 3D backdrops.
- Interactive controls and labels adopt higher tracking (`0.02em` to `0.06em`) and medium-to-bold weights to guarantee legibility across fluctuating cinematic lighting and translucent glass backplates.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-hybrid stage model designed to frame the WebGL canvas without cluttering the interactive viewport:
- **Cinematic Stage Layer:** The full-viewport canvas (`100vw`, `100vh`) anchors the 3D world.
- **HUD Frame:** Persistent controls (sound toggle, perspective switcher, chapter timeline) anchor to the outer margin safe zones: `3rem` on desktop, reducing to `1.25rem` on mobile.
- **12-Column Responsive Grid:** When structured story sheets or donor impact modules unfold over the scene, content conforms to a centered 12-column grid (maximum container width: `1280px`) with `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Spatial Densities:** Vertical spacing prioritizes breathing room, allowing character animations to occupy the upper or lateral thirds of the camera frustum without UI collision.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics optical physical light rather than artificial drop shadows:
- **Atmospheric Glassmorphism (Level 1 - Floating HUD & Bubbles):** `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`, surface fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.76)`, bordered by a razor-thin top-lit highlight (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`) and an ambient tinted drop shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(43, 38, 33, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(216, 90, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Raised Interactive Elements (Level 2 - Primary Actions):** Opaque warm saffron and teal pill elements with an internal ambient rim: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`.
- **Golden Solar Halo (Interactive State):** Focused and hovered interactive components emit a sun-drenched aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 183, 56, 0.35), 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(216, 90, 42, 0.35)`.
- **Cinematic Vignette:** A permanent low-opacity radial gradient around the viewport corners (`rgba(43, 38, 33, 0.12)`) guides visual attention toward the central third where Bharari navigates.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of `3` (Pill-shaped) defines the visual vocabulary. 
- Interactive elements (buttons, sound controls, search pills, timeline scrubbing nodules) utilize pure organic capsules (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- Narrative dialogue balloons use generous curvilinear hulls (`2rem` / `32px` corner radiuses) accented by a directional organic teardrop tail pointing toward Bharari's beak.
- Cards, modal viewports, and chapter plates embrace `rounded-xl` (`3rem` / `48px` on desktop, `1.5rem` / `24px` on mobile), reinforcing the non-threatening, child-friendly, animated storybook physical quality.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Pill:** Background filled with `#D85A2A`, text color `#FFFFFF`. Features an inner top highlight border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`), pill radius, and smooth kinetic hover state that elevates the button 2px while casting a warm golden solar bloom (`#F5B738`).
- **Secondary Glass Pill:** Translucent white backplate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`), text `#1A7B9B`, with a crisp translucent sky-teal border (`rgba(26, 123, 155, 0.25)`).
- **Icon Controls (Sound Toggle / Camera Reset):** 44x44px circular glass disks with dynamic waveform micro-animations indicating soundscapes (ambient village birds, wind chimes, flute score).

### Speech Bubbles (Story Dialogue)
- **Construct:** Floating speech bubbles placed in 3D projection coordinates near Bharari. Backplate composed of frosted milk glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), with gentle terracotta-tinted text (`#2B2621`) and saffron-highlighted keywords.
- **Tail:** Rounded organic SVG pointer oriented dynamically toward the character’s current screen position.

### Story Progress & Chapter Indicators
- **Scrubber Bar:** A slender 4px translucent trough positioned along the bottom margin. The active progress track gleams with a saffron-to-gold gradient (`#D85A2A` to `#F5B738`).
- **Chapter Waypoints:** Small circular pearl nodes along the timeline that expand into pill-shaped chapter titles on hover or when triggered by 3D camera milestones.

### Cards & Story Panels
- Overlaid informational sheets feature deep `rounded-xl` corners, subtle cream-to-white vertical gradient fills, warm terracotta headings, and crisp imagery with soft inner borders simulating physical storybook pages.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Inputs
- **Inputs:** Floating pill forms with soft cream fills (`rgba(243, 239, 230, 0.7)`), inset shadow for subtle depth, and golden glow rings on focus.
- **Selection Controls:** Circular radio indicators and organic rounded checkboxes styled in deep monsoon teal (`#1A7B9B`) with crisp white iconography.
